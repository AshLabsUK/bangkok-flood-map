Live readings: floodbangkok.bangkok.go.th (BMA road water-level sensors). When that feed is down, roads also draw from Longdo Traffic / iTIC agency reports (DOH, BMA Drainage Dept.) and Traffy Fondue citizen reports (unverified) — refreshed here every 3 min. Road geometry: OpenStreetMap contributors, via the Overpass API. Rain radar: RainViewer. Rain gauges & river/canal levels: ThaiWater (HII, RID, TMD, BMA…).
Road/district names are machine-translated from the source Thai and may not match official signage. Reports without a BMA sensor code have no exact district/depth — only a severity classified from the report text.

Unofficial — not affiliated with BMA. Data may be delayed, missing or wrong. BMA flood hotline 1555.
